# PM-14 Pump Motor Maintenance Manual

Use the pump duty point, suction conditions, and run history to interpret
motor and pump readings. The sample equipment identifier is PM-14.

## PMP-LF1 low flow warning

PMP-LF1 indicates low measured flow while the pump has a run command. Record
suction and discharge pressures, valve position and the flow trend in L/min.
Compare the reading with the expected duty point. A closed valve, restricted
inlet and erroneous flow signal can produce similar indications.

## Motor cooling and winding temperature

Inspect the temperature trend and indicate whether fan airflow changed.
Compare winding RTD readings at comparable load and ambient conditions.
Electrical maintenance should review repeat winding alarms and suspected
insulation issues using the approved site procedure.

## Vibration follow-up

Record velocity in mm/s RMS at the same locations and axes used for baseline
collection. Compare vibration alongside flow and pressure to check whether
the trend is associated with a change in operating conditions.
