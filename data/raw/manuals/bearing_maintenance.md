# SKF-6205 Bearing Maintenance Guide

This sample guide describes a bearing position in the BRG-17 training asset.
Identify the suffix on the fitted bearing. In the sample inventory, 6205-2RS/C3
denotes a sealed variant with C3 clearance.

## BRG-VIB2 and vibration trend

BRG-VIB2 is a bearing vibration alarm. Compare drive-end vibration velocity in
mm/s RMS with the same measurement direction, speed and load used at baseline.
Inspect trend history for a step change after alignment or lubrication work.
An overall vibration reading alone cannot identify a particular bearing defect.
Record frequency data and bearing housing temperature for follow-up.

## Lubrication and temperature

Record the lubricant identity, last service time and installed bearing type
before interpreting a fast temperature rise. In this sample fleet the sealed
6205-2RS/C3 variants are handled through the bearing replacement procedure.
Where the asset has a serviceable bearing, follow the approved lubrication
schedule and applicable quantity for that exact installation.

## Handover

Escalate repeat alarms, increasing noise, or a sustained increase in vibration.
Keep the measurement point and operating conditions in the work order.
