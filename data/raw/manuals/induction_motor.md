# MX-200 Induction Motor Maintenance Manual

The MX-200 in this sample fleet is a three-phase induction motor. Match every
reading to the equipment identifier, load and temperature before comparing it
with a historical baseline. The nameplate and approved site procedure govern
service decisions.

## MTR-TH1 winding temperature alarm

MTR-TH1 indicates a winding temperature alarm. Record the winding RTD in degrees
Celsius, current on all three phases and whether the alarm occurred at startup
or after sustained operation. A trend that rises with load can indicate overload.
A rising trend at steady load can point to obstructed cooling or a fan fault.
If indicated temperature jumps abruptly, check the RTD circuit and compare
against an independent reading before treating it as a confirmed overheat.

## Phase current imbalance

Compare phase currents at the same load. Uneven phase current can accompany
supply imbalance, a connection problem or a winding fault. Record all three
values and the corresponding supply measurements for electrical maintenance.
Repeated protective trips require escalation through the site procedure.

## Post-maintenance records

Record the work performed, ambient conditions, phase currents and stabilized
winding temperature. Collect the follow-up trend at a comparable load.
