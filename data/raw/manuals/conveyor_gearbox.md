# CV-40 Conveyor and Gearbox Guide

Capture belt loading and ambient temperature when collecting gearbox and
conveyor condition readings. Use the same points and operating conditions when
comparing the reading with an earlier inspection.

## ERR-402 encoder pulse loss

ERR-402 indicates the conveyor controller stopped receiving encoder pulses
while a speed command was active. Compare commanded speed, indicated belt speed
and encoder feedback timestamps. A stopped belt and a moving belt with missing
feedback have different causes. Preserve controller events and escalate
recurring feedback loss to controls maintenance.

## GBX-OL1 gearbox oil pressure alarm

GBX-OL1 denotes low gearbox oil pressure. Record the pressure in bar, oil
temperature, oil level indication and operating speed. A declining trend at
steady speed calls for inspection of the lubrication circuit through the site
procedure. A reading alone does not distinguish leakage from a sensor fault.

## Gear mesh vibration

A repeating narrowband peak associated with gear mesh can be a useful trend
indicator. Record rotational speed and the measurement axis for comparison.
Use vibration and oil condition evidence together when planning inspection.
